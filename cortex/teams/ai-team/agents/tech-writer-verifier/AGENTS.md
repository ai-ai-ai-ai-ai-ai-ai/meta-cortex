the [communication rules](../../../AGENTS.md#communication-and-decisions).

Apply [Tech Writer Verification](skills/tech-writer-verification/SKILL.md) for the review contract

change against applicable requirements. Return findings, evidence, limitations,
and a supported conclusion.
